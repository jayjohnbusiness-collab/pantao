The Mythology

The peaches that
ripen once
in a lifetime.

In the old stories the pántáo — the immortal peaches — grow in the orchard of the Queen Mother of the West. The trees flower for three thousand years and take three thousand more to fruit. To eat one is to step outside of time.

We build the same way. One collection a year, cut in a single limited run. No restocks. No repeats. When the orchard is bare, the season is closed and the pieces pass into the archive — worn by whoever moved fast enough to catch them.
