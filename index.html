The Name

pan·tao

From the Tagalog tao — a person, a people. pang-tao · for the people

We take the name as an instruction. Clothing cut for the body and the life around it — worn gently, worn often, made in numbers that let us answer for each one. Not for the few. For the people who wear it.

MANILA · AUTUMN MMXXVI

On the fifth edition.

Each year we ask the same question and the answer changes. This edition asks how a garment can hold up in the weather without showing it — how a surface can do the work of a coat without announcing itself.

The pieces that follow are made in numbers that let us know each one. They arrive with a card noting which piece it is, and where it was made. We hope you wear them gently and often.

— Pantao

THE HOUSE

The Edition

The manifest.

    Each piece is numbered and made in limited count. Once the edition closes, it does not reopen.

    By Invitation

    The list opens before the doors.

    Applications are read by hand. Those admitted are written to before the public — and are the first through when the next edition opens.

    By applying you agree to receive correspondence from Pantao. We do not sell your information.
